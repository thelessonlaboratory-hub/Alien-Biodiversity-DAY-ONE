INCOMING TRANSMISSION
EXPEDITION CHIEF XENOZOOLOGIST

DR. KAELO

Hello Researcher! I've been looking forward to working with you!

Our expedition has documented 20 species of animal-like organisms here on Lathonerth. These 20 species belong to two distinct genera, which we've named Mupal and Hoopler. Within the groups, though, some of the creatures look surprisingly similar, which makes identifying individual species a bit of a challenge.

Fortunately, I've developed a dichotomous key that will help you distinguish them. Your mission is to examine specimens carefully, follow the key, and record your identifications in the expedition's Field Guide.

You can choose which organisms to investigate. Before continuing with your expedition, you must identify at least two Mupals and two Hooplers. Any additional organisms that you have time to identify will be added to your Field Guide, which will make future ecology research tasks easier. So try to identify as many as you can!

Remember: sometimes the smallest details make the biggest difference when identifying an unfamiliar species.

Are you ready to meet some more of Lathonerth's inhabitants?
